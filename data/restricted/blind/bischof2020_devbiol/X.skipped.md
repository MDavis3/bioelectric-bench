# bischof2020_devbiol - pass O skipped arms and notes

Rows: Fig 7A re-cut arms of octanol-induced (127 uM, 3 d) worms with heads at both ends, re-cut at day 7 or day 49
(re-cut round 2). Coded as `combination` (octanol history + surgery). Counts from Supplementary Dataset 1 sheet "Figure 7"
(per-trial %, n = 15 per trial stated) converted per trial and summed; several per-trial percentages are not multiples of
1/15, so true n likely varied (n_total nominal). Remainder (non-DH) = single-headed, stated by the methods' two-category
scoring ("counting number of single-headed and double-headed regenerates"). Day-49 midway halves have identical values for
"primary" and "secondary" half in the dataset (heads indistinguishable at day 49) -> one row.

Skipped arms:
1. Fig 7B side-wound outgrowths (day 7 facing primary head 39/42 head; facing secondary head 37/42 tail; day 49 38/41 head):
   numbers exist, but head/tail identity of a lateral outgrowth fits no outcome family (planaria_ap_polarity categories describe
   whole-fragment polarity; other_family expected/unexpected would need an extractor-defined expectation). Logged, not extracted.
2. Initial octanol double-head induction (127 uM, 3 d, pharynx and pre-tail fragments): no induction rate reported.
3. All cilia-flow collision-zone experiments (Figs 1-6, S2, S6-S8: irradiation, internal tissue removal, 3% ethanol, CMC,
   primary/secondary/double decapitation, continuous decapitation, head-tip removal, half-head removal, brain bisection,
   VNC cuts): continuous measures (collision-zone position), not categorical anatomical outcomes; agents are not
   electrical/ionic apart from the shared octanol history.
4. Brain size (Fig 4, S5) and pharynx position (Fig S3): continuous measures, not rows.
