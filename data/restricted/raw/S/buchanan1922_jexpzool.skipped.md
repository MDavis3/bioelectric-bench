# Skipped arms and problems: buchanan1922_jexpzool (pass S)

Article read: PDF pages 17-64 of the whole-volume scan (tables are scanned images; Tables 1A, 2A, 3A, 4A, 5A read from page images).

Rows: 90 = 30 series x 3 pieces (A, B, C), every control set and treated series printed in the head-frequency tables (1A chloretone, 2A chloroform, 3A ether, 4A chloral hydrate, 5A ethyl alcohol). Table values are percent; converted to counts with n = 50 pieces per flask (methods text; all values are even numbers). Head classes folded into normal / headless / other (other = teratophthalmic + teratomorphic + anophthalmic, described in notes of each row). Table 1A series 9, piece A prints only 90% (44 normal, 46 teratophthalmic, dead 0): flagged partial, remainder unstated. Table 5A series 1 (6/28/1917), piece C prints only 92% (78 normal, 14 teratophthalmic, dead 0): also partial. Table 1A series 8 B (98%) and C (102%) are rounding-level and left complete.

Other experiments exist but have no numbers.
- Ethyl alcohol at mol 1/4 to 1/5 and other strengths: deaths too many, no data given.

- Methyl, normal propyl and iso-butyl alcohol head-frequency experiments: described in words only.
- Whole-animal oxygen-consumption controls: not a regeneration arm.
- Abstract in Spanish (PDF p. 17): translation, no data.
