# rustia1925_jexpzool (J. Exp. Zool. 42:111-141, PDF pages 128-159)
Rows extracted: water controls (Tables 1, 6, 8) and HCl arms (Tables 2, 7, 8), one row per arm per body level where the biaxial-head cell is nonzero; plus KOH text arm (18% biaxial tails, no n). HCl and KOH are classed as ion_substitution (closest type; the paper itself treats them as general depressants/pH change). All rows partial (remainder unstated).
Skipped:
- Ether (Table 3), chloretone (Tables 4, 7, 8), low temperature (Table 5), chloral hydrate, alcohol, caffeine (text only, no numbers), and the 7 mm / six-piece chloretone series: general anesthetics or temperature, no electrical or ionic component.
- Blank (zero) cells in all tables: no rows (a zero-count row cannot be represented); levels A-J with no biaxial heads are therefore absent for each arm.
- Table 2 'Average' row (pool of the optimum-exposure series): not a row, its members are rows.
- Table 6/7 series A-10, A-15 (water, no biaxial heads) and Table 7 series with no HCl: none.
- Biaxial tails in controls (text: 1 in nearly 8000 pieces, 3 in nearly 3500, none in P. maculata): totals only across all arms, not a distinct arm.
- Figures 17 and 18 duplicate table averages (not read).
Note: body levels A-J treated as distinct arms (different cut level); series with different hours (e.g. 81 vs 82 h, 83 vs 85 h) kept as separate arms.
