Row decisions

- Head classes mapped: normal -> normal; acephalic/headless -> headless; teratophthalmic + teratomorphic + anophthalmic -> other (breakdown in notes). Dead pieces -> dead_count (the paper's percentages include the DEAD column).
- Partial rows: chloretone M 1/400 5 h A piece (series 9; reported values sum to 90%), ethyl alcohol M 1/10 24 h C piece (series 1; sum 92%). No stating sentence.
- Minor rounding: chloretone series 8 B sums to 98%, C to 102% (left as given).
