## What changed and why
- Nigericin (13%) and monensin (12%): the legend N (132, 89) is the N of the Fisher's exact test comparing treated with control. The paper does not say whether it counts the treated arm only, so the conservative reading keeps percentages with n_total null and records N in the notes. B had converted with N as the treated n.
- Nigericin remainder: the paper says the remaining share was morphologically normal, so normal 87 is the paper's own number. Monensin remainder: the paper does not describe it, so it goes to `other`. B had assumed normal; A had left it blank.
- K+ gluconate 1-week presoak: the text gives "N = 60, of regenerated treated worms", which is a treated-arm n, so 5/60 was converted. The remainder goes to `other`, because it is not described. The presoak concentration is not stated.
- Ethanol vehicle arms (0.0024%, 0.0011%) and the presoak control: kept from B as normal 100 / double_head 0 percentages with low outcome confidence. The paper states only 0% double-headed and shows single-headed control regenerates. n is not stated. A had skipped them.
- Dropped (A only): beta-catenin dsRNA + nigericin, n=22. That n is the size of a morphometric group of double-headed worms picked for shape analysis, not a scored denominator, so it gives no outcome rate.
