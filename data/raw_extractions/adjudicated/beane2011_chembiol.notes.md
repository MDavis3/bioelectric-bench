- Fig 4B3 (midline sagittal, 0% headless, n=42): kept from B. A skipped it. The 42 non-headless go to `other`, because the paper does not say whether they are normal or cyclopic.
- Fig 5 re-wounding of SCH-pretreated headless regenerates: kept in the A/P family as SCH-lineage rows, which both passes agreed on. Stage text was changed so it carries no outcome words.
