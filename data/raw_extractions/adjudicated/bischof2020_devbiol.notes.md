## Why no rows
- The only ionic agent is 127 uM octanol, used once (3 d on pharynx and pre-tail fragments) to make the double-headed animals used throughout. The paper reports no induction rate for it.
- The count-type outcomes (Figure 7A cut-plane re-cuts: 87% vs 31%, 29% vs 31%, 84% SH, 96% SH, 97% DH; Figure 7B side outgrowths 39/42, 37/42, 93%) compare mature vs immature double-headed animals and cut positions. The arm factor is age and surgery, with no ionic agent in the arm; octanol is a shared background step weeks earlier. They also do not fit planaria_cryptic, which is for normal-looking regenerates. Kept out as the conservative reading, as both passes decided. If the project later decides that octanol-derived re-cuts should be combination rows, these are the arms to add (n stated only for the 39/42 and 37/42 side-outgrowth arms).
- Ethanol, CMC, irradiation, decapitation and nerve-cord cuts: non-ionic, with continuous cilia-flow readouts.
