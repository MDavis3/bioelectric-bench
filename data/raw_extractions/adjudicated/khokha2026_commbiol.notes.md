## Agreed and kept

Species is Xenopus tropicalis, as the paper states.

## Dropped (B only)
- Fig 8C-D pitx2 in situ arms (DMSO, DIDS, DIDS + choline, DIDS + choline + ouabain). The text gives percentages and N, but pitx2 is a molecular marker of LR identity at ~stage 25, not organ placement. Marker readouts are excluded here as in the other adjudicated papers. B also split the pitx2 absent/bilateral classes into "other", which would mix a non-anatomical readout into the family.
