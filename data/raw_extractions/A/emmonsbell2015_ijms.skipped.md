# Skipped arms and problems: emmonsbell2015_ijms (pass A)

Paper: Emmons-Bell et al. 2015, IJMS, doi 10.3390/ijms161126065

## Extracted
- Octanol (8-OH) pre-tail arm, morphometric subsample breakdown (Fig 2A legend): one row, n=31.
- Water control, WT G. dorotocephala regenerated 10 days (Fig 2A legend, n=8): one row.
- Octanol arm, remodeled subgroup at day 30 (Fig 6I legend, n=6): one row.

## Skipped arms
- Full octanol-treated cohort (n>243, Fig 1E-H and Fig 3B): the species-shape frequencies appear only as bars in Fig 3B. The text gives no numbers, and the figure cannot be read as text, so no bar-chart values were entered.
- 6-OH hexanol control (Fig S1B, Results 2.1, Discussion 3.1): described as "had no effect" with no numbers in the text. Supplement not available.
- Intact worms soaked in 8-OH for more than 3 days (Results 2.1): "did not exhibit any unusual morphological outcomes", no numbers.
- Wild-type reference species (G. dorotocephala, D. japonica, P. felina, S. mediterranea) in Fig 2A, Fig 4A-C, Fig 5A-C, Fig 6A-D: unmanipulated reference groups, not electrical or ionic arms.
- Brain length/width ratio (Fig 4D-G; pseudo GD n=4, pseudo DJ n=4, pseudo SM n=6): continuous ratio, not a planaria_head_shape category.
- Neoblast counts in anterior 1/6th (Fig 5D-G; pseudo groups n=7, 8, 7): continuous counts, not a category.
- Isopotential region counts from DiBAC (Fig 6I, control n=7, pseudo GD n=5, pseudo SM n=5, pseudo DJ n=5, pseudo PF n=2): continuous per-animal counts of voltage domains, not a planaria_head_shape category.
- Procrustes distances and CVA (Table 1, Fig 2A): continuous shape statistics.
- Agent-based model (Fig 8, Fig 9, Table 2, Fig S2): in silico, not an experimental arm.
- Day-by-day remodeling time course (Fig 7, Results 2.7): descriptive only, no numbers.

## Problems
- The octanol arm has n>243 in the figures but the category breakdown given in text is only the morphometric subsample (n=31). The row uses n_total 31 to match the counts. Outcome confidence is low.
- Fig 3B frequencies could not be read, so the full cohort's species-shape distribution is not recorded.
- Supplementary material (Fig S1A, S1B) not retrieved: supp folder is empty. The 100% fidelity claim for the water control is from the text, and its n is not stated in the text.
- Fig 2A morphometric group sizes are used as outcome counts. Their selection process is not described; they may not be a random sample of the 243 treated fragments.
