# Skipped arms and problems: pai2012_development (pass A)

Paper: Pai et al. 2012, Development, doi 10.1242/dev.073759

## Extracted
- GlyR mRNA + ivermectin, dorsal injection (Fig 2A text): 1 row.
- EXP1 mRNA, dorsal injection (Fig 2A text): 1 row.
- GlyR + ivermectin with 60 mM choline chloride (Fig 2C text): 1 row. Baseline 0 mM is the same arm as the GlyR + ivermectin row, not duplicated.
- Controls, uninjected / lacZ / GlyR-only / ivermectin-only (Fig 2A text, qualitative): 1 row, low confidence.
- DNKir6.1p, all four blastomeres (Fig 3A text): 1 row.
- GFP / lacZ mRNA controls, all four blastomeres (Fig 3A text, qualitative): 1 row, low confidence.
- DNKir6.1p, dorsal two blastomeres (Results text, 16% ectopic, data not shown): 1 row.
- DNKir6.1p, ventral two blastomeres (Results text, 20% ectopic, data not shown): 1 row.
- Kv1.5 + DNKir6.1p co-injection (Results text, 2% ectopic and 11% defect): 1 row.

## Skipped arms
- DNKir6.1-ER retention mutant (Fig 3A): text gives only the total of 35% eye-related phenotypes, with no split into endogenous defects or ectopic eyes. Cannot be placed in the xenopus_eye categories.
- GlyR + ivermectin or EXP1 injected into ventral blastomeres (Fig 2A): described as "no significant effect", no numbers.
- GlyR + ivermectin at 20 mM choline chloride (Fig 2C): intermediate value only in the bar chart; not in text.
- Nav1.5 with sodium gluconate series 10, 15, 20, 40 mM (Fig 3N): ectopic eye values only in the figure; text says only the 15 mM condition induced ectopic eyes. No numbers in text.
- Table 1 suppression screen (Verapamil, gap junction and serotonin blockers, Kv1.5 background): table is an image in the HTML; no values in text. Text says verapamil "significantly reduced" induction, with no numbers.
- Table S2 (other ion channels inducing ectopic eyes) and supplementary Fig S2/S3: supplement not retrieved; text gives only "a majority".
- Dominant-negative Pax6 (Fig 2E): membrane voltage pattern disruption (76%, n=25) is a bioelectric readout, not an eye-anatomy category, and text gives no eye count for this arm.
- Fig 2D in situ marker expression (Rx1, Pax6, Otx2 disrupted or normal fractions, n=7 to 26): molecular marker expression, not anatomical eye categories. Not recorded.
- Fig 1 hyperpolarized cluster (CC2-DMPE, Vmem ~10 mV): physiological measurement, no outcome counts.
- Fig 4 histology and immunostaining of ectopic eyes: qualitative descriptions, no counts.
- Fig 4C in situ (Otx2, Pax6, Rx1 ectopic expression): qualitative, no numbers.
- Fig 1 and Fig 2B-C photographs: images only.

## Problems
- No PDF or supplement in the folder (supp index only, 5.2KB html). Table 1 and Fig 2A/3A bar values are not in the text and could not be read.
- n is not stated for the Fig 2A, Fig 3A percentages (Fig 3A legend says n=5, probably experiment batches). n_total is null throughout.
- For percentages reported as "disrupted" or "eye-related" only, the normal count is the complement, derived by the extractor and flagged in notes (rows for GlyR + ivermectin dorsal, EXP1 dorsal, 60 mM choline chloride, DNKir6.1p all blastomeres, Kv1.5 co-injection).
- Control rows (uninjected, lacZ, GFP, GlyR-only, ivermectin-only) recorded as 100 normal from qualitative text. These are placeholders, not measured percentages; confidence low.
- Dorsal and ventral DNKir6.1p rows report ectopic eye incidence only (text, data not shown). Normal and defect proportions are not given.
- Text reports "approximately" and "~" values (25%, 20%, 7.5%). Recorded as given.
