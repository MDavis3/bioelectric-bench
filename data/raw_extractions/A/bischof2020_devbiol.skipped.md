## Why there are no rows
- The paper's only electrical/ionic agent is 127 uM octanol (gap-junction blocker). It is used only to generate double-headed (DH) animals (Methods, "Double-head induction": 3 days in octanol, then regeneration). It is applied as one shared induction step for all DH animals, not as a varied experimental arm, and the paper gives no outcome counts for octanol-induced DH formation.
- All other manipulations are non-ionic: irradiation (200 Gy), decapitation, half-head removal, brain bisection, internal tissue removal, lateral/perpendicular nerve-cord cuts, nerve-cord deviation, 3% ethanol (cilia disruption, a flow-assay control with no anatomy count), 2% carboxymethylcellulose (viscosity). Per the instructions, surgical or drug arms with no electrical/ionic component are not rows.
- Ethanol and CMC arms give continuous collision-zone and flow measures, not anatomy counts.
