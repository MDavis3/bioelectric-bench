# Skipped arms and problems: beane2013_development (pass A)

Paper: Beane et al. 2013, Development, doi 10.1242/dev.086900

## Extracted
- Smed-H,K-ATPase RNAi pharynx regenerates (Fig 2A): one row, percentages only.

## Skipped arms
- Control dsRNA (Venus-GFP) arm: named in Methods, but the paper gives no numeric shrunken-head or headless count for it in the text or legend.
- M50054 apoptosis inhibitor (Fig 6A): not an electrical or ionic intervention, so not a row. The paper reports 45% reduced remodeling versus 0% control (n=40); not extracted.
- Staurosporine (apoptosis inducer, Fig S7/S8 text): not electrical or ionic; no categorical counts.
- Smed-H,K-ATPase RNAi marker analyses (Fig 3A-D: synapsin, sFRP-1/Wnt11-5, Innexin-9, cintillo, H3P): arrowhead presence/absence calls with n only; cintillo dot averages (40.2 vs 13.7) are continuous and not mappable to the planaria_ap_polarity categories.
- Smed-H,K-ATPase RNAi pharynx and head size time series (Fig 1, Fig 4A/B): continuous percentages of worm area; not categorical outcomes.
- Smed-H,K-ATPase RNAi H3P and caspase-3 counts (Fig 5, Fig 6B): proliferation and apoptosis measures, not anatomical outcome categories.
- Intracellular pH and membrane voltage (DiBAC4(3)) assays (Fig 2B, S4): physiology readouts, no anatomical categories.
- Behavioral movement quantification (Fig S6): not anatomical outcome categories.
- Other H,K-ATPase candidates (v31.000529, v31.001001, v31.021250, v31.002299, v31.000996): described in Methods in words only; no numeric counts.
- Dugesia japonica H,K-ATPase inhibition (SCH-28080) and supplementary Fig S3: referenced to Beane et al. 2011 without numbers in this paper.

## Problems
- Supplementary material: supp folder is empty. Only the PMC supplementary index (720B html) is listed, and the supplementary PDF (supp_140.2.313_DEV086900.pdf) was not retrieved. Supplementary Figures S1, S3, S7 and S8 may contain counts that were not checked.
- Fig 2A gives percentages (4.2% headless, 91.6% shrunken head) with n>=125 stated only as a lower bound. n_total is null. The remaining ~4.2% of regenerates are not described, so the outcome counts do not sum to 100. Confidence on outcomes and n_total is low.
- Fig 2A dsRNA timing: injected days 1-3 and cut on day 8 or 14, with no single amputation day in the text. timing_start_h left null.
