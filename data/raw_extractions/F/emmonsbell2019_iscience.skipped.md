3. BaCl2-adapted worms returned to water for 30 days then re-exposed to 1 mM BaCl2 (Fig 1B): "all worms" degenerated - words only, no n.
4. BaCl2-adapted worms in water (Fig 4Ba) - words only.

6. BaCl2-adapted worms kept in BaCl2 without AMTB (Fig 4Bc): "showed no degeneration" - words only.
7. Long-term BaCl2 (15-37 days): head regrowth described in words only.
8. Valinomycin 100 nM + 15 mM K-gluconate (Fig S1): voltage-dye readout only, no anatomical outcome.
9. BaCl2 30 min DiBAC imaging (Fig 3): voltage-dye readout only.
