# Q017 v0 (draft for red team): allele direction within channel/pump genes, human ClinVar

Status: DRAFT, not sealed. Written before ClinVar was downloaded. The planner has seen the database test v1 results
(loss of function, mouse/zebrafish: FAIL) and the exploratory human HPO results (gene level: class excess explained by
annotation density). It has not seen any variant-level or mechanism-split data.

## Question

Most direct bioelectric evidence comes from gain of function or pharmacological depolarisation, not loss. In humans,
do conditions caused by pathogenic MISSENSE variants (a proxy for gain-of-function / dominant-negative action in channel
genes) carry more morphological patterning phenotypes than conditions caused by TRUNCATING variants (loss of function)
of the SAME genes, and is this missense-over-truncating excess larger for channel/pump genes than for matched
membrane-protein control genes?

## Data

- ClinVar variant_summary (GRCh38 rows; germline; clinical significance Pathogenic or Likely pathogenic; review status
  at least "criteria provided, single submitter"). Molecular consequence from the variant name/type: truncating =
  nonsense, frameshift, canonical splice (+/-1,2), whole-gene or exon deletion; missense = single amino-acid
  substitution. In-frame indels and others excluded.
- Each variant's condition(s) via OMIM/Orphanet IDs in PhenotypeIDS, mapped to HPO annotations through phenotype.hpoa.
- Endpoint: the human patterning set used in the exploratory secondary analysis (harness/db_secondary.py ROOTS), to
  be frozen and reviewed before outcomes.
- Classes: config/db_classes_v1.json (sealed). Blocks: smallest HGNC family (as v1).

## Unit and contrast

- Unit: gene x consequence (missense, truncating) for genes with at least one eligible condition for BOTH consequences.
- Y = 1 if any condition linked to that gene x consequence has a patterning HPO term.
- Model: logistic Y ~ missense + missense:class + gene fixed effects (or conditional logistic within gene), plus
  log number of HPO terms of the linked conditions as an annotation-density covariate (lesson of Q019).
- Primary statistic: the missense x class interaction (ratio of odds ratios), one-sided, block bootstrap and block
  permutation of class labels.
- SESOI: ratio of odds ratios 2.0 (to be argued by the red team).

## Worries

- Missense is not gain of function: many missense variants are loss of function; dominant-negative is common in channels.
- Ascertainment: missense channelopathies are found through seizures/arrhythmia; truncating variants through
  developmental clinics. Conditions differ, not just mechanism.
- Few genes have both consequences with conditions.
- Annotation density per condition.
