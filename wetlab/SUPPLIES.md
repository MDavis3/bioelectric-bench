# Desk Lab Kit v1: supplies (for Manav to buy himself; nothing has been bought)

Prices are rough (search snippets, 2026-11-10; wetlab/SCOUT_NOTES.md has the sources). Check each before buying.

## Worms (decide first; see READINESS.md "Species decision")

| Option | Supplier listing seen | Rough price | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Girardia (Dugesia) dorotocephala, "brown" or "black" planaria | Ward's Science; Flinn; Carolina (via Fisher) | about $14-17 per class of ~30; about $42-53 per 100 | The only species in our corpus with a published octanol protocol that hobby suppliers sell (Emmons-Bell 2015, head-shape readout). Ask the supplier which species they actually ship: listings disagree on which is "brown". |
| Girardia (Dugesia) tigrina, "brown" planaria | Flinn | about $17 per 30 | No qualifying published positive control in our corpus. |
| Dugesia japonica / Schmidtea mediterranea | not sold to individuals | n/a | D. japonica is the only species where a published polarity positive control qualifies under the sealed rule (octanol). Would need an academic lab to share a clonal line. |

Order at least 200 worms: one piece is kept per worm, so rig validation uses 60 worms and batch 2 another 90, plus spares to settle in for 1-2 weeks first.

## Chemicals (only for batch 2, only after batch 1 passes)

| Item | Purpose | Hazard (read your bottle's safety data sheet) |
|---|---|---|
| 1-octanol, reagent grade, smallest bottle | published positive control (gap-junction blocker) | eye irritant; combustible; harmful to aquatic life. Gloves, glasses, ventilation. Never pour treated water down a drain or outside without checking local rules. |

1-octanol is sold by lab suppliers, some of which do not ship to residences; check before ordering. Nothing else in the kit is a hazardous chemical. No other drug is used at home: SPEC.md allows only interventions with published planarian handling, and every dose comes from the cited paper.
