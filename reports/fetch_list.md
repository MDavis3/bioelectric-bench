# Fetch list: papers and supplements for Manav (2026-11-10)

**Why:** the kill criterion needs at least 25 planarian polarity papers with counts. 9 are in hand. These files decide whether the question is testable at all.

**How:** use legal routes only: your library login, interlibrary loan for the old journals, or the author's or publisher's page. Don't use shadow libraries; anything from them would contaminate the record.

**Where to put files:** `inbox/<number>/` in this project folder, for example `inbox/7/marsh1947.pdf`. Any file name is fine. The folder is git-ignored, so nothing you download gets pushed. If a paper turns out to have no numbers, or you can't get it, write a one-line `inbox/<number>/note.txt` (for example "no access" or "only drawings, no counts"). That resolves the paper in the corpus too.

**Priority:** start with A1 and B1. Those are most likely to add papers with counts, and new lab lineages and decades.
